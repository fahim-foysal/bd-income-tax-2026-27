# Privacy policy — Bangladesh Income Tax 2026-27 (Claude plugin and skill)

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy covers the `bd-income-tax-return` plugin and skill published at https://github.com/fahim-foysal/bd-income-tax-2026-27, in both editions: the full edition on the `main` branch and the no-code edition on the `directory` branch.

## What the plugin reads
To work out your tax, Claude reads only what you choose to share in your conversation. This can include your name, TIN, salary, bank statements, investment certificates, assets and liabilities.

## What the plugin stores
Nothing. The plugin has no server, database, account system, analytics or tracking. It keeps no copy of anything you share.

## What the plugin sends
Nothing. It makes no network calls and has no MCP servers, connectors or hooks.
- **No-code edition:** contains only instructions, tax rules and worked examples.
- **Full edition:** adds a Python calculator that runs on your own machine, reads a figures file you or Claude create, and prints the result. It sends nothing anywhere.

If you ask Claude to help you on the NBR e-Return site (etaxnbr.gov.bd), Claude uses its own browser tools, and you log in and pay yourself. That site's own privacy terms apply there.

## Your conversation
Your conversation with Claude, including any documents you upload, is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and your Claude account settings. The author of this plugin cannot see your conversations.

## Contact
Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/fahim-foysal/bd-income-tax-2026-27/issues.

# Tax deducted / collected at source — rates an individual meets (2026-27)

Use this to check a TDS certificate, a bank's interest deduction or a payslip, and to gross up a net receipt. In the return, TDS is a **credit** (not final) unless noted.

Sources:
- Withholding Tax Rules 2026 (উৎসে কর বিধিমালা ২০২৬), SRO 210-Law/Income Tax-1/2026, 8 June 2026, in force 1 July 2026: https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/rules/SRO-210,_08_Jun,_2026.pdf
- Income Tax Act 2023 as amended by the Finance Act 2026
- NBR Aykor Nirdeshika 2026-27

An amendment to the rules was gazetted 5 July 2026: https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/rules/With_holding_2026.pdf. It is in Bijoy font and could not be read. If a rate below decides the answer, check that PDF or the certificate itself.

## Income a salaried or professional person receives
| Payment | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Salary (s.86) | Employer deducts the estimated annual tax ÷ 12 | ITA s.86 |
| Bank / NBFI interest, savings, FDR, DPS profit (s.102) | 10% with TIN / return proof, 15% without | Guide p.17, FAQ |
| Sanchayapatra profit | 10% (credit, taxed at slab) | Guide p.61-62 |
| Dividend to an individual (s.117) | 15%. Exempt only if the dividend is exempt under the 6th Sch. | Finance Act 2026 s.72 |
| Lottery, game, prize, online-game winnings (s.118) | 25% | Finance Act 2026 s.73 |
| Club membership fee (s.137A) | 10% collected by the club | Finance Act 2026 s.80 |
| Consultancy / professional / technical service fee paid by a company, govt body or other specified person (s.90) | **15%** for an individual (7.5% for others) | Rules 2026, r.4 items 1-3 |
| Meeting fee, training fee, honorarium (s.90) | **20%** | Rules 2026, r.4 item 7 |
| Indenting commission | 7.5% | r.4 item 6 |
| Payment to a non-resident (s.119) | Rule 5 table (e.g. legal fee 20%, commission 15%) | r.5 |

## When an individual sells or buys
| Transaction | Collected | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Land transfer (s.125), Table-1 | Higher of **5% of deed value** or a fixed amount per decimal (শতক). The fixed amount depends on mouza and land class; e.g. Gulshan/Banani/Motijheel/Tejgaon Tk 3-9 lakh per decimal | Rules 2026, r.6 Table-1 |
| Building / flat / apartment / floor space | Higher of a per-sq-m rate or a % of deed value: classes Ka–Gha Tk 800/sq m or 8%; class Ngo Tk 500/sq m or 6%; elsewhere Tk 300/sq m or 6% | r.6 |
| Gift deed | Gift tax collected with the deed (Gift Tax Act 1990) | Finance Act 2026 s.76 |
| Selling gold, silver, jewellery, gems or platinum to a dealer (s.112A) | **0.5%** of the price, deducted by the buyer | Finance Act 2026 s.70 |
| Private car registration/fitness (s.153) | Car AIT table, see `rules-2026-27.md` §5 | Finance Act 2026 s.85 |
| Developer-flat capital gain | 15%, payable over 3 years | Guide p.48 |

Tax collected on a land transfer is a credit against the tax on the capital gain (guide p.51, p.94). It is not the final tax.

## Grossing up a net payment (Rules 2026, r.12)
If someone paid "tax-free" (the payer bore the tax), the gross amount is

    gross = 100 × net / (100 − rate)

Show the gross figure as income and the deducted tax as a credit. Example: an honorarium paid as Tk 80,000 net at 20% → gross 1,00,000, credit 20,000.

## Checks worth doing
- Bank interest TDS at 15% instead of 10% usually means the bank lacks the TIN. Credit the actual amount and tell the taxpayer to submit the TIN.
- A TDS claim needs a certificate (r.10) or challan. The e-Return verifies only car AIT, iBAS++ salary TDS and sanchayapatra automatically. Other claims are checked later by the tax officer (guide p.151).
- Under s.163(3) and (4), only TDS under ss.138-139, and the TDS of people exempt from filing, is final tax. Everything else here is a credit.

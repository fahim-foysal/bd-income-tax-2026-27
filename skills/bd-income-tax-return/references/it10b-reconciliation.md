# IT-10B / IT-10BB reconciliation (net-wealth method)

## The identity
```
Sources of fund   = total income (taxable) + tax-exempt income (incl. the salary 1/3 exemption)
                  + gifts and other receipts (gifts from relatives, gifted jewellery, loans received,
                    non-income credits)
Outflows          = lifestyle expenses (IT-10BB, including tax paid during the year)
                  + gifts/donations given + losses
Change in wealth  = net wealth at end − net wealth at start
Difference        = Sources − Outflows − Change in wealth   →  must be 0
```

## How to build it
1. **Closing assets** (30 June): every bank balance (from statements), MFS balances, cash in hand, DPS (installments paid to date), sanchayapatra, shares/mutual funds at cost (include money paid but not yet allotted), gold at cost, vehicles, property, loans given. Liabilities: loans taken.
2. **Opening net wealth**: last year's filed IT-10B; for a first return, the same items on the previous 30 June (bank balance after the last interest/TDS posting of that day).
3. **Sources**: income figures from the computation; list every gift/other receipt separately with a reference (date, bank, account, who it came from).
4. **Expenses**: start from what the taxpayer knows (rent × 12, family support transfers visible in the statement, education, travel), and let food/household absorb the balance — then sanity-check it's plausible per month.
5. **Cash in hand** is the usual unknown. Use the taxpayer's honest estimate at both dates. Changing it moves the expense figure one-for-one; it never changes tax.
6. Re-check: any asset bought partly with cash withdrawn earlier → that cash is now in the asset, so expenses go down by the same amount.

## Things that commonly break it
- A bank account or MFS wallet left out of closing assets.
- DPS installments after 30 June counted as an asset or investment.
- Investment money that left the bank on 30 June but whose units/certificate came in July — it is still wealth on 30 June; show it.
- Gift of jewellery entered as an asset but not as a receipt.
- TDS left out of IT-10BB.
- Rounding: the site uses whole taka, so round each line once and let the cash or food line absorb ±1.

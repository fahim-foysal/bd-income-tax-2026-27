# Rules for tax year 2026-27 (income year 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026)

Sources: NBR "Aykor Nirdeshika 2026-2027" (return guide), Finance Act 2026 (Act 96 of 2026), Income Tax Act 2023 (ITA). Page numbers refer to the NBR guide. **Use these only for 2026-27.** The Finance Act 2026 fixes the individual slabs for five years: 2027-28 uses the same slabs, but 2028-29 onwards changes them (Finance Act Schedule 2, para Kha/Ga). Write a new rules file for those years.

Machine-readable copy: `rules/2026-27.json`. All 32 worked examples in the guide are encoded in `examples/nbr-guide-2026-27.json`, and the calculator reproduces every one (`--selftest`).

## 1. Slabs [p.11, p.56] and thresholds
| Slice of total income | Rate |
|---|---|
| First 4,00,000 (or the category threshold) | 0% |
| Next 3,00,000 | 10% |
| Next 4,00,000 | 15% |
| Next 5,00,000 | 20% |
| Next 20,00,000 | 25% |
| Rest | 30% |

The tax-free first slab depends on the taxpayer [p.1, p.11, p.57]:
| Category (`category` in the calculator) | Threshold |
|---|---|
| general | 4,00,000 |
| woman, senior (65+ years) | 4,50,000 |
| third_gender, disabled | 5,25,000 |
| freedom_fighter (gazetted war-wounded), july_fighter (gazetted July 2024 wounded) | 5,50,000 |
| Parent or legal guardian of a disabled child | +50,000 **per child** (`disabled_dependents`). If both parents file, only one may claim it [p.57] |

The other slab widths stay the same: a woman's 10% slab covers 4.5L–7.5L. Resident (and non-resident Bangladeshi) individuals only. Other non-residents pay 30% flat.

## 2. Income heads

### Salary (s.32-34) [p.20-31]
- **Private sector, and anyone not on a government pay scale:** all receipts are employment income (basic, allowances, bonuses, perquisites, employer's contribution to a recognised PF). Exempt amount = lower of **1/3 of receipts** or **Tk 5,00,000** (6th Sch. Part 1 para 27).
- Perquisite values [p.21-22]: employer-paid housing = annual rental value. Car: ≤1500cc Tk 15,000/month; ≤2000cc 20,000; ≤2500cc 30,000; above that 50,000.
- Not employment income: business travel/daily allowance actually spent, group insurance premium paid by the employer, and reimbursed group-insurance medical costs [p.20]. Unspent travel allowance is income.
- **Government pay-scale employees** (SRO 225-Law/Income Tax-07/2023) [p.23-27]: only **basic pay, festival allowance and bonus** are taxable. Allowances listed in the SRO (house rent, medical, conveyance, Bangla New Year allowance, etc.) and lump grant are exempt. **No 1/3 exemption.** This covers the National Pay Scale, Public Bodies, the bank/insurance/finance company and police/BGB/judicial pay orders, and the Joint Services Instructions. It also covers staff of Sonali, Janata, Agrani, Rupali, BDBL and BASIC Bank from 2017-18 onwards. Anything outside the SRO list is fully taxable: lecture fees, honoraria, foreign-trip allowances [p.29], and the pay of MPO teachers, who count as private [FAQ 42]. Calculator: `"govt_pay_scale": true`.

### Rent / house property (s.35-39) [p.32-36]
Income = annual rent − allowable costs + special rent income.
- Repairs: **25%** of rent (residential), **30%** (commercial).
- Plus municipal/city tax, land revenue, interest on a bank/NBFI loan for the property, and insurance premium. If only part of the building is let, apportion these by the let share. Repairs are not apportioned.
- Special rent income: **10% of the year-end balance of an adjustable advance**; the full amount of any non-refundable premium (may be spread over the lease or 5 years).
- Rent of **Tk 25,000+/month** must come into a bank account, or a penalty of 50% of the tax on that income (min Tk 5,000) applies. Business rent must be paid through a bank to be deductible.

### Agriculture (s.40-44) [p.36-42]
- **Para 20:** up to Tk 5,00,000 of agricultural income is exempt, **only if** the person has no income other than agriculture and financial assets.
- **Para 20A:** up to Tk 5,00,000 exempt for poultry, shrimp/fish hatcheries, pelleted poultry/fish feed, dairy products, frog farming, seed marketing, sericulture. One 5L cap covers all 20A income. Having any 20A income disqualifies the para 20 exemption [p.40].
- Tea/rubber grown and processed: 60% agriculture, 40% business.
- Without books: costs allowed at 60% of market value of the produce [p.42].
- Both exemptions are **lost if the return is filed late** [p.80].

### Business or profession (s.45-56) [p.42-47]
Net profit after allowable expenses and depreciation (3rd Schedule). Watch these limits: entertainment is capped at 4% of profit; pharma free samples at 2% of turnover up to 10 crore (1.5% up to 20 crore, 1% above); perquisite payments to employees are capped at 25%; any expense paid without the required withholding tax is disallowed. Interest accrued but unpaid for 3 years becomes income. A shortfall in opening capital within 5 years of self-assessment is income.

### Capital gains (s.57-61) [p.47-52] and dividend
| Kind (`special_rate_income.kind`) | Treatment |
|---|---|
| `cg_short`: asset held ≤ 5 years (incl. land) | added to slab income |
| `cg_long`: held > 5 years (incl. land, also money received above the deed value) | 15% |
| `cg_listed_securities`: listed shares/units | first Tk 50 lakh exempt, rest 15% |
| `cg_developer_flat`: flats/cash from a developer under a land-development contract | 15% on (value received − land cost); payable over 3 years |
| `cg_gold_jewellery`: personal gold, silver, jewellery, gems, antiques, art, club membership | **5%** |
| `dividend` (individual) | 15% |

Personal cars, furniture and clothes are not capital assets. Special-rate income is outside the rebate base. The rebate still comes off the total tax [p.50].

### Financial assets (s.62-65) and other sources (s.66-69) [p.52-54]
Bank/FDR/DPS interest, sanchayapatra profit and bond interest are taxed at **slab rates**, with TDS credited [p.61-62, p.104]. Gross up any receipt to the pre-TDS amount. Other sources include royalties, honoraria, tuition, government cash subsidies, joint-venture distributions, and lottery winnings (taxable in the year won). Loans of more than Tk 5 lakh taken from individuals in cash (not crossed cheque or bank) and not repaid within 6 years become income.

### Firm share [p.54-55]
Include a partnership share in total income. Relief = tax × (firm share / total income). The firm share is also left out of the rebate base. Calculator: `other_income` item with `"head": "firm_share"`.

### Spouse / minor children [p.55]
Include their income in the taxpayer's if they are not separately assessed.

### Income deductions
A donation to the **Center for Zakat Management** is deducted from income (6th Sch. Part 2), not treated as an investment [p.64]. Calculator: `income_deductions`.

## 3. Investment rebate, s.78 [p.58-67]
Rebate = lowest of:
- (a) **3%** of total income excluding exempt, reduced-rate (special-rate), firm-share and final-tax income
- (b) **10%** of eligible investment
- (c) **Tk 7,50,000**

Eligible investments [p.12-13, p.59-60]:
- Life insurance premium (policy in Bangladesh) or contract-based deferred annuity
- GPF/RPF contributions (employee + employer); approved superannuation fund
- Welfare fund and group insurance contributions
- DPS / monthly savings scheme, **max Tk 1,20,000/yr**
- Government securities / sanchayapatra, **max Tk 5,00,000** (new purchase)
- New investment in listed shares, mutual funds, ETFs and unit certificates
- Universal pension scheme (any amount)
- Zakat fund
- Donations to bodies on the guide's list, including NBR-approved charitable hospitals and disability organisations, icddr,b, CRP, the Liberation War Museum, Ahsania Mission, BRAC and Thalassaemia Foundation

**Not eligible:** FDR, laptops/consumer goods, the DPS amount above the cap, and anything paid outside the income year [p.64]. Cash a rebated investment (sanchayapatra, unit fund) before maturity and the rebate is clawed back. Investment encashed within the same year earns no rebate [p.66].

## 4. Minimum tax [p.58] and turnover tax [Finance Act s.163]
- If total income exceeds the threshold, tax after rebate is at least **Tk 5,000**. For a **new taxpayer** it is **Tk 1,000** [p.67]. Location doesn't matter.
- **Turnover tax** on business/profession: if the regular tax on business income is below the turnover tax, turnover tax applies. Rates: 1% of gross receipts (general); tobacco 3%; carbonated/sweetened beverage 2.5%; mobile operators 1.5%; 0.2% for the first 3 years of commercial production for manufacturers.
  - Not applicable to commission business, DO business, money changers, gold/silver/jewellery/gem trade, or govt import of fertiliser/seed/essentials.
  - Excess turnover tax carries forward. Exempt or reduced-rate sources get a proportional cut.
  - The calculator compares turnover tax with the slab tax share attributable to business income.

## 5. Surcharge, car AIT, environmental surcharge [p.71-77]
**Surcharge** (individuals) is a % of the tax on income, computed before the rebate. This matches every guide example: p.92 on 92,400, p.96 on 3,04,000.

| Net wealth (IT-10B) | Rate |
|---|---|
| ≤ 4 crore | 0, but 10% if the person owns more than one car (EVs count) or house property over 8,000 sq ft |
| > 4 crore – 10 crore | 10% |
| > 10 – 20 crore | 20% |
| > 20 – 50 crore | 30% |
| > 50 crore | 35% |

If the income tax is nil, the surcharge is nil. Tobacco manufacturers pay an extra 2.5% of that business income.

**Car advance tax** (s.153), paid at registration/fitness renewal:
| Engine | AIT |
|---|---|
| ≤1500cc | 25,000 |
| ≤2000cc | 50,000 |
| ≤2500cc | 75,000 |
| ≤3000cc | 2,00,000 |
| ≤3500cc | 2,50,000 |
| ≤4500cc | 4,00,000 |
| above | 5,00,000 |
| Microbus / double-cabin pickup | 40,000 |

| EV motor | AIT |
|---|---|
| ≤200 kW | 25,000 |
| ≤300 kW | 50,000 |
| ≤400 kW | 75,000 |
| above | 1,00,000 |

Each car beyond the first pays 50% more. The car AIT is credited against tax, but if it exceeds the regular tax the excess is **not refundable**: tax = max(regular tax, car AIT) [p.77].

**Environmental surcharge**: for each car other than the one with the lowest rate.
| Engine | Surcharge |
|---|---|
| ≤1500cc | 25,000 |
| ≤2000cc | 50,000 |
| ≤2500cc | 75,000 |
| ≤3000cc | 1,50,000 |
| ≤3500cc | 2,00,000 |
| above | 3,50,000 |

**Electric cars are exempt from 1 July 2026.** It is collected at fitness renewal and is neither refundable nor adjustable.

"Motor car" excludes buses, trucks, pickups, human haulers, CNG autorickshaws and motorcycles.

## 6. Filing time [p.4-5, p.70, p.78-82]
Individuals file self-assessment returns (s.180) from 1 July to **30 June** of the tax year. The incentive or extra is a % of the **tax payable u/s 173**, i.e. after rebate, TDS and advance tax.

| Return filed | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 Jul – 30 Sep | Incentive: 5% of s.173 payable, max Tk 25,000 |
| 1 Oct – 31 Dec | Neither incentive nor extra |
| 1 Jan – 31 Mar | Extra: higher of 2% or Tk 3,000 |
| 1 Apr – 30 Jun | Extra: higher of 5% or Tk 5,000 |
| After 30 June (late, s.174) | Extra: higher of 10% or Tk 5,000 |
| Late, after a s.212(1) notice | Extra: higher of 15% or Tk 10,000 |

On a **late return**, exemptions are lost except 6th Sch. Part 1 paras 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 17, 35 and the salary exemption. The surviving paras cover:
- Government pension
- Gratuity up to 2.5 crore
- Recognised/approved fund receipts
- PF interest
- Govt voluntary-retirement payments
- Legal foreign remittance
- Inter-family gifts shown in both returns
- Zero-coupon bonds
- Wage Earner and other premium bonds

Agriculture, para 20A, freelancing/IT and listed-gain exemptions are lost. The rebate is still allowed [p.80-81]. Interest on late filing is 50% higher. Other first-time filers may file by 30 June after the income year. People abroad for study, work or earning get 90 days after returning to Bangladesh. If a deadline falls on a holiday, it moves to the next working day.

A revised return u/s 175 can be filed within 180 days, once. It cannot lower the tax of the original return.

## 7. Advance tax, credits, refunds [p.82-84]
- Advance tax (s.154) is due if the last assessed income exceeds **Tk 10 lakh**, excluding capital gains and one-off income. Pay it in 4 equal instalments by 15 Sep, 15 Dec, 15 Mar and 15 Jun.
- TDS and AIT are credited against tax with certificates/challans. Rates and the gross-up formula: `withholding-tax-2026-27.md`. Bank interest TDS is 10% with a TIN, 15% without. TDS deducted under ss.138-139, and all TDS of people exempt from filing, is final.
- A refund can be claimed or carried forward to next year (s.163). Earlier years' refunds can be adjusted against this year's tax.

## 8. Exempt income checklist [p.84-87; 6th Schedule]
These are not added to total income, but must be shown in the return:
- Govt pension; govt gratuity up to 2.5 crore
- Recognised PF/superannuation/gratuity fund receipts; PF interest
- Govt voluntary-retirement payments
- Pensioners' savings certificate interest, where total investment ≤ 5L
- Employer reimbursement for business
- Trust/HUF share already taxed; legal foreign remittance
- Wage-earner and premium bond income; zero-coupon bonds
- CHT indigenous people's income from CHT activities
- Agriculture / 20A (see above); 1/3 salary exemption
- Freedom-fighter welfare allowances; govt and foreign govt awards
- Old-age-home income
- Gifts from spouse/siblings/parents/children shown in both returns
- Listed-share gains up to 50L; group insurance receipts
- **ICT/freelancing/content creation** (list of 23 services incl. freelancing, content creation, call centre, BPO) to 30 June 2027, received 100% through the banking channel
- **SME manufacturing** income: turnover up to 70L for women/disabled owners, 50L otherwise; must be registered with the SME Foundation [p.65-66, p.86]

## Points to verify case by case
- **Salary paid from abroad**: the legal-remittance exemption (para 17) may apply. Confirm the conditions with the user. A local bank transfer from a local employer does not qualify.
- **Gifts**: money or gold from close relatives is exempt only if both returns show it [p.80]. Gifts sent from abroad through a bank are exempt even if the donor doesn't file. Keep a gift note and the sending-account record.
- **Donations**: the e-Return has a Rebate → "Others/SRO" line and a separate "Donation (6th Schedule Part-2)" screen. Enter each donation in one place only.
- **Ambiguities the guide leaves open** (say so to the user):
  - Whether the Jan–Jun extra floor applies when the s.173 payable is nil.
  - Exactly which business tax figure is compared with turnover tax. The guide's p.105 example uses a figure that matches neither the full nor the proportional slab tax, but both are far above the turnover tax there.
